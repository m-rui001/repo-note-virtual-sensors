# Novelty check: `D_min(F)` / `Phi(F) = tr(Theta P_inf(F))`

## Verdict: **partially-anticipated** (the object is classical; the *use* as a named "reachable cost floor of a sensing subspace" was not found)

Both ingredients of `D_min(F)` are textbook and individually ancient. (1) The steady-state LQG average cost separating as `J = tr(W P_c) + tr(Theta P_est)` — the perfect-information term plus an estimation term — is the separation principle written numerically; it is the literal objective of Tzoumas–Carlone–Pappas–Jadbabaie (arXiv 1709.08826 / 1802.08376, ACC 2018 / IEEE TAC 2020), who minimize `sum_t tr(Theta_t Sigma_{t|t}(S))` against the "noiseless perfect state-information" reference problem. (2) The filter Riccati with measurement-noise covariance set to zero is a named classical object — "**singular filtering**" (Moylan 1974; Shaked, IEEE TAC 1985, DOI 10.1109/TAC.1985.1103784, "Explicit solution to the singular discrete-time stationary linear filtering problem"; Osburn–Bernstein ACC 1994 on the generalized eigenvalue problem for the *singular* discrete-time Riccati; Abooshahab–Alyaseen–Bitmead–Hovd, Automatica 137:110017, 2022; the Int. J. Control 2022 "limit Kalman filter" paper explicitly lists "the limiting form of the Kalman filter when the measurement noise covariance tends to zero and the related singular filtering problem Shaked (1985), Moylan (1974)" as the same open thread). The small-observation-noise limit is also a classical singular-perturbation topic (Marchetti, Asymptotic Analysis 1994). So `P_inf(F)` is not new, and `Phi(F)` follows from it plus the decomposition in one or two lines — that is the realistic kill shot a reviewer can deliver.

What I could **not** find, in any channel, is: (i) the precision/information matrix `Gamma` released as a free variable *at fixed `F`* and the resulting infimum taken and named; (ii) the resulting residual `tr(Theta P_inf(F))` used as a subspace score / "cost of the sensing architecture independent of rate"; (iii) the detectability-else-`+infinity` convention. The closest functional precedents are: **Tanaka–Esfahani–Mitter** (arXiv 1510.04214), which states the *full-state* floor (`D = tr(W S)` is "the best achievable control performance when unrestricted amount of information about the state is available") and designs the sensor pair `(C,V)` via `SNR = C V^{-1} C^T` — i.e. your `Gamma` — but never restricts the subspace; **Tanaka–Sandberg** (arXiv 1503.01848), which jointly designs `(C_t, V_t)` and even selects `r_t = rank(P_{t|t-1} − P_{t|t})`, i.e. the *dimension* of the sensed subspace, but only under an information price (it explicitly says the unpriced problem is trivial: "y_t = x_t (perfect observation) together with the LQR is optimal"); **Zardini–Censi–Frazzoli** (arXiv 2011.10758, ECC 2021), which in the *scalar* LQG hardware-co-design case literally computes `lim_{q0 -> 0} P_track(q0)` — the perfect-sensor cost floor — as a Pareto-front endpoint; and the Skelton "information architecture" line (**Li–de Oliveira–Skelton 2008**, **Goyal–Majji–Skelton 2021**, arXiv 2011.10838) where sensor precision = inverse noise intensity is an optimization variable with a price/budget, and "all states available for measurement with no noise" is treated as a named special case.

Recommendation: do not present `Phi(F)` as a new quantity. Present it as the **evaluation of the classical singular-filtering (noise-free measurement) Riccati solution inside the textbook LQG decomposition**, and claim only the specific use: a rate-free, SDP-free, one-Riccati-solve lower bound on the reachable cost of a sensing subspace, and its role as a subspace score. The most damaging single item is **Borggaard–Burns–Zietsman (ACC 2011)**: they already use `tr(P)` from an observer/filter Riccati for a *noise-free* sensed output as a cheap one-Riccati-solve surrogate for the LQG cost in sensor placement, explicitly contrasted with the expensive full LQG metric — so "Riccati-trace metric instead of the LQG cost, for sensor placement" is published; your delta is the `Theta` weighting, the infimum-over-`Gamma` interpretation, and the connection to the directed-information feasible set. Note also the likely trivialization: with `R_meas = 0` the update annihilates the `row(F)`-known directions, so `P_inf(F)` is essentially a Lyapunov/observability-Gramian object on `null(F)` — expect a reviewer to say "this is just the covariance of the unmeasurable directions of `(A,F)`".
